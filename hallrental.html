Rent Pfiefer Hall & Our Outdoor Pavilion

Moose Lodge 2010 offers a spacious and welcoming venue for private parties, celebrations, meetings, fundraisers, and community events.

🎉 Pfiefer Hall

Our large indoor hall is ideal for birthdays, anniversaries, memorial gatherings, business meetings, club events, and private celebrations.

Features: large open room, stage, dance floor, tables, seating, and access to lodge amenities.

🌴 Outdoor Pavilion

Our outdoor pavilion is a great space for casual gatherings, cookouts, social events, and Florida-style celebrations.

Features: covered outdoor area, BBQ-friendly setting, relaxed atmosphere, and plenty of room to gather.

📍 Great Location

Conveniently located near I-95, PGA Boulevard, The Gardens Mall, hotels, restaurants, and local businesses.

Benefits: easy access, safe neighborhood, and plenty of parking.

✅ Perfect For

Wedding receptions, birthday parties, celebrations of life, meetings, charity events, dances, banquets, and community gatherings.
