About Moose Lodge 2010

Moose Lodge 2010 in Palm Beach Gardens is a welcoming place for members, families, and guests to gather, relax, and enjoy a variety of activities. Our lodge offers a comfortable and friendly environment where everyone feels at home.

🏠 Social Quarters

Our social quarters provide a relaxed atmosphere to unwind with friends. Enjoy beverages, conversation, and regular lodge activities in a comfortable setting.

🎱 Game Room

The game room features 6 TV's, pool tables, shuffleboard, darts, backgammon, and Left-Right-Center making it a great place to spend time with fellow members. Cornhole, pingpong are available for setup in the Pfiefer Hall. Horseshoes by the BBQ pavillion.

♠️ Card Room

Moose Lodge 2010 features a private card room with two full-size poker tables and a TV. Members free enjoy games in a private comfortable atmosphere.

A 7-card stud game is played Saturday's at 11:00 AM. Bring your own cards, chips, dominos, majohng set or board game to play when the room is vacant.

🎉 Pfiefer Hall Rental

Our spacious hall is available for events and private functions. Whether you're hosting a party, celebration, or community event, the lodge provides a great venue for gatherings.

🍔 BBQ & Outdoor Area

Members can enjoy our covered outdoor BBQ and seating areas, perfect for cookouts, social events, and enjoying Florida weather.

🤝 Community & Events

Moose Lodge 2010 regularly hosts events including dinners, bingo nights, social gatherings, and more. We are proud to be an active part of the local community.
